# Role module: generative AI and transparency (Art. 50)

For people who use generative AI (text, image, audio, video, code) or AI
systems that interact directly with people.

> Art. 50 applies from the date in the app's legal dates configuration; a
> transition period applies to marking obligations for systems already on
> the market.

## 1. Transparency duties

- **Interacting with people (Art. 50(1))**: people must be told they are
  interacting with an AI system unless it is obvious.
- **Machine-readable marking (Art. 50(2))**: providers of generative systems
  mark synthetic output so it is detectable as AI-generated.
- **Emotion recognition / biometric categorisation (Art. 50(3))**: inform
  the people exposed.
- **Deep fakes (Art. 50(4))**: disclose that image, audio or video content was
  artificially generated or manipulated.
- **AI-generated text on matters of public interest (Art. 50(4))**: disclose
  unless it underwent human review and editorial responsibility is taken.

## 2. Labelling content: our practice

Follow the labelling rules in the internal AI usage policy. When in doubt,
label. Keep the disclosure clear and visible at the latest at first
exposure.

## 3. Good practice with generative AI

- Verify facts, figures, quotes and references.
- Check outputs for bias, stereotypes and inappropriate content.
- Respect copyright and confidentiality; do not enter personal data into
  unapproved tools.
- Keep a human responsible for anything published or sent externally.
