# Awareness: people affected by our AI systems

Art. 4 asks us to consider the persons on whom AI systems are used. This
module helps you see the system from their side.

## Who is affected?

Depending on the system: customers, job candidates, employees, patients,
students, tenants, citizens, or members of the public.

## What can go wrong for them

- **Wrong decisions** they cannot easily see or contest (e.g. a rejected
  application).
- **Unequal treatment**: the system works worse for some groups.
- **Loss of privacy**: data used in ways they did not expect.
- **Deception**: not knowing they are dealing with an AI or with synthetic content.

## What you can do

1. Be able to explain, in plain words, how AI was involved in an outcome.
2. Make sure people know how to ask for human review or complain.
3. Treat complaints about AI-assisted outcomes as signals, not noise.
4. Where the law requires it, inform people that AI is used (e.g. Art. 26(11), Art. 50, Art. 86 right to explanation).

## Reflect

For the AI systems in your path, write down: who is affected, what is the
worst plausible mistake, and how would that person find out and get it fixed?
