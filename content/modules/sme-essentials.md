# SME essentials: AI at work in 20 minutes

A condensed programme for small organisations. It covers the core module
in short form. Role modules are added only where the systems you use need
them.

## The five rules

1. **Use approved tools only.** The list is in our AI usage policy.
2. **Protect data.** No personal or confidential data in tools not approved for it.
3. **Check before you use.** AI makes things up and can be biased. Verify facts, figures and sources.
4. **Be transparent.** Tell people when they talk to an AI or see AI-generated content where required.
5. **Speak up.** Report errors, odd behaviour or complaints to the AI contact person.

## What AI is good and bad at

Good: drafting, summarising, translating, sorting. Bad: facts it cannot look
up, rare situations, explaining itself, knowing when it is wrong.

## Who is affected?

Think of the customers, applicants or others our AI tools touch. If a
mistake would hurt them, check twice and keep a human in charge.
