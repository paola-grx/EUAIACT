# Role module: deploying high-risk AI systems (Art. 26)

For people who operate or take decisions with a high-risk AI system that the
organisation deploys (for example in recruitment, credit, education or
access to essential services).

> Annex III high-risk obligations apply from the date in the app's legal
> dates configuration. Preparing early is strongly recommended.

## Deployer duties in practice

1. **Use according to the instructions for use** of the provider (Art. 26(1)).
2. **Assign human oversight** to people with the necessary competence,
   training, authority and support (Art. 26(2)).
3. **Input data**: where you control it, ensure it is relevant and
   sufficiently representative for the intended purpose (Art. 26(4)).
4. **Monitor** operation; if there is reason to believe use presents a risk,
   inform the provider or distributor and the market surveillance authority
   and suspend use (Art. 26(5)).
5. **Keep logs** under your control for at least six months (Art. 26(6)).
6. **Inform workers** and their representatives before using a high-risk
   system at the workplace (Art. 26(7)).
7. **Inform affected persons** that they are subject to the use of a
   high-risk system when it makes or assists decisions about them (Art. 26(11)).
8. **Data protection impact assessment** where required, using the provider's
   information (Art. 26(9)).
9. **Fundamental rights impact assessment** where Art. 27 applies (e.g.
   public bodies, credit scoring, life and health insurance pricing).

## Your role

Know which of these duties your job carries out, and who in the organisation
owns the others. Escalate if you believe a duty is not being met.
