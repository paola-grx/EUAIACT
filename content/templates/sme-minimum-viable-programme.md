# Minimum viable Article 4 programme for small organisations

A lightweight programme for organisations with few staff and mostly
off-the-shelf AI tools. It is a starting point, not a guaranteed standard:
Article 4 asks for measures appropriate to your context.

## Step 1 - List your AI tools (30 minutes)

Add every AI tool staff use at work to the **AI systems** page, including
chatbots, copilots, meeting transcription and AI features in existing software.
Mark tools that generate content, and any used on customers, applicants or employees.

## Step 2 - Link people to tools (30 minutes)

For each person, link the tools they use and their role. Most people will be
*casual users*. Record a short needs assessment for each person.

## Step 3 - Issue a one-page AI usage policy (1-2 hours)

Use the *Internal AI usage policy* template. Record it in the evidence
register as "Internal AI policy issued".

## Step 4 - Run the SME essentials module (20 minutes per person)

Switch on **SME mode** in Settings. Everyone gets the *SME essentials*
module; role modules are added only where a tool requires them (e.g. a
high-risk use or content generation).

## Step 5 - Hold one awareness session per year (1 hour)

Discuss real examples from your work: what went well, what went wrong.
Record it in the evidence register as "Awareness session held", with attendees.

## Step 6 - Keep it current

- Default refresh: every 12 months (Settings).
- When you add a new AI tool, link the people who use it; the app assigns
  re-training automatically.
- Issue the *Article 4 Measures Statement* once a year and keep it on file.
