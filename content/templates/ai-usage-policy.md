## 3. Rules for everyone

1. Check AI output before using it; you stay responsible for your work.
2. No personal, confidential or customer data in tools not approved for it.
3. Label AI-generated content where required (see section 5).
4. Do not use AI to make decisions about people without the oversight set out for that system.
5. Report errors, incidents, suspected bias and complaints to [AI contact point].
