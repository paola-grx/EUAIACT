Checklist

  1. List every AI tool in AI systems, including chatbots and copilots.

  3. Record a short needs assessment for each person.
  4. Adapt the AI usage policy template and record it in the evidence register.
  5. Share each person's personal learning link.
  6. Hold one awareness session a year and record it.
  7. Issue the Article 4 Measures Statement from the dashboard once a year.
