Guidance: limits of automation in this app

This app uses simple, transparent rules to help you run an Article 4 programme. Every automated output is a proposal that a person reviews. Here is what each feature can and cannot do.

A suggested class stays marked unconfirmed until an administrator confirms it on the system page, and unconfirmed classes appear as gaps on the dashboard.

Tailored learning paths

  • Paths are built from the recorded role(s), the linked AI systems and the needs assessment. Each module shows the reasons it was selected.
  • The rules cannot see how people actually work. If a path looks wrong, correct the inventory or the assessment rather than working around it.
  • Depth levels (foundation / standard / advanced) are guidance for the learner, not a grading of the person.

Knowledge checks

  • Quizzes are for learning reinforcement only. Article 4 requires measures, not a guaranteed individual level, so there is no pass mark.
  • The evidence register records that a check was taken, never the score.

Coverage figures and re-training triggers

  • Coverage shows who has current training measures. It measures activity, not competence.
  • Re-training is triggered by inventory changes, material content updates and the refresh cycle. It depends on the inventory being kept current.

Records and statements

  • The AI Literacy Training Record and the Article 4 Measures Statement document measures taken. They are not certificates and do not attest to competence or legal compliance. The AI Act prescribes no AI literacy certificate.

Legal dates

  • Dates come from a configuration file that maintainers must verify before each release. See legal dates.
